What predicts future pressure performance?

We built ratings on 2022–24 and tested them on 2025–26 (rank correlation, higher is better). Pressure-only form did not carry over; normal-level quality did. That is why the rating leans 80% on baseline quality and 20% on pressure evidence.

Method choices: opposition-quality weights (full members 1.0, Ireland and Zimbabwe 0.7, associates and A-teams 0.4); byes and leg-byes assumed to be one run each; 40–90 players per backtest, so results are noisy.
